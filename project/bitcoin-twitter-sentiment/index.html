High-Level Summary

This academic project scores Bitcoin-related social-media sentiment and compares daily sentiment trends with Bitcoin price data. The notebook uses AFINN sentiment scoring and time-based aggregation to explore whether sentiment and price move together.

The Challenge

Social posts are noisy and informal, and sentiment changes over time while market prices fluctuate continuously. The analysis needed to convert text into consistent sentiment labels and align the resulting series with price observations.

Architecture & System Overview

The notebook loads sentiment and Bitcoin price CSV files, maps AFINN scores to positive, neutral, and negative labels, aggregates sentiment by day, and compares the resulting series with price data.

Key Engineering Trade-offs & Lessons

  • A lexicon-based method is transparent and lightweight but can miss sarcasm, context, and crypto-specific meaning.
  • Daily aggregation makes trends easier to compare but hides intraday changes.
  • The notebook describes exploratory association, not evidence that sentiment causes price movement.
