Restaurant Voice Ordering AI

Adds phone-based speech input and spoken responses to restaurant ordering, with escalation to staff when automation needs help.

Speech RecognitionDeepgramNLPText-to-Speech

High-Level Summary

This voice-ordering system lets restaurant customers place phone orders through spoken conversation. I extended the text-ordering workflow with speech recognition, restaurant vocabulary adaptation, spoken responses, and a transfer path to restaurant staff.

The Challenge

Phone ordering introduces recognition errors from accents, background noise, menu terminology, quantities, and ambiguous instructions. The system needed to reuse the existing order-understanding logic while preserving a human fallback when a call could not be completed confidently.

Architecture & System Overview

The call channel converts speech to text, sends the transcript through the restaurant-ordering NLP system, and converts the response back to speech. Calls that need human assistance can be transferred to restaurant staff.

My Specific Contribution & Technical Ownership

  • Extended the text-ordering product to support phone-based conversational orders.
  • Adapted speech recognition using restaurant text, menu terms, food vocabulary, and varied accents.
  • Connected call handling, transcription, order interpretation, and spoken responses.
  • Added a fallback path for transferring customers to restaurant staff.

Key Engineering Trade-offs & Lessons

  • Reusing the text-ordering engine avoids duplicating menu and order logic, while speech recognition introduces a separate source of uncertainty.
  • A human transfer path preserves customer choice and supports calls that are noisy, ambiguous, or outside the bot flow.
  • Real-time voice interactions balance conversational speed with confirmation and staff transfer for ambiguous orders.
