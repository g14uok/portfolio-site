Restaurant Text Ordering AI

Converts natural-language SMS and web orders into menu selections and POS-ready payloads, with uncertain cases routed for human review.

PythonNLPTF-IDFMachine LearningPOS APIs

High-Level Summary

This conversational ordering system converts informal SMS and web-chat messages into structured restaurant orders. I built domain-specific NLP and order-validation components that map menu language to POS-ready data and route uncertain orders for human review.

The Challenge

Restaurant customers use misspellings, abbreviations, modifiers, and menu-specific language that generic text processing can misinterpret. The system needed to translate those requests into exact menu items, apply pricing rules, and avoid sending uncertain orders forward without review.

Architecture & System Overview

Text preprocessing and menu-specific matching identify items, quantities, and modifiers. Business rules produce an order payload, while a supervised correctness classifier can route uncertain cases to human review before POS integration.

My Specific Contribution & Technical Ownership

  • Built modular Python NLP logic for restaurant-specific menus, abbreviations, misspellings, substitutions, and modifiers.
  • Mapped unstructured messages into structured order payloads for POS systems.
  • Applied text normalization, stemming, lemmatization, vectorization, and TF-IDF-based matching.
  • Developed supervised order-correctness classification to identify uncertain orders for human review.
  • Integrated order interpretation with backend APIs and restaurant POS workflows.

Key Engineering Trade-offs & Lessons

  • Domain-specific NLP offers control over menus and business rules but requires restaurant-specific vocabulary and maintenance.
  • Human review adds a step for uncertain orders but protects customer experience when automated interpretation is unreliable.
  • Classical NLP and supervised models were selected for a product built before commercial LLMs were broadly available for this use case.
